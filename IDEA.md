# SPACE CUSTOMS?

## High Level

A game where you are in charge of maintaining the law around a jumpgate/spacelane.
It involves operating a craft/instruments in order to scan ships in the area for illegal modifications/cargo and other non-compliance.
It might involve communicating with the ship directly.
The player may need to dispatch patrol assets to check things or otherwise enforce the rules.

## The Rules

Examples of rules could be:

- Military components cannot be used unless registered as a military vehicle.

- Weapons/specific defensive subsystems are prohibited unless specific licences are acquired.

- Components from specific manufacturers are banned.

- Certain components operating outside of specification is prohibited. eg. Overdriving engines, reactor past safe limits.

- Cargo manifest must be present and match actual ship contents. 


## Story beats?

- Strong AI is banned and limited AI can only be used in an assistive role.
  Perhaps the sheer number of rules and time pressure could force the player to overly rely on it, and it could be taken away to highlight the dependance.

- The AI could be covertly modified to ignore certain violations and they could recieve a tip off and manually discover the issues, uncovering more plot.

- Corruption discovered and the player must decide how to act on it. Enforce the laws, raise the issues?

- Player could be moved between different areas as the game progresses, from quiet backwaters to busy space-lanes.

- Player could start with their AI assistant damaged/inactive and have to do checks manually. It's managable but time consuming.
  Ramp up the difficulty but then introduce the assistant. Maybe make a level that's super easy with the assistant where before they would have struggled.


## Interface

I think the game could be played primarily by a normal point and click/button interface, with some typing involved.

Left side of the screen could have information panels about rules, module spec readouts, etc.
Right side could be information about the target track(ship), sensor readouts, cargo and crew manifests.

Bottom (or top?) Could be buttons and taskbars that track active processes like scanning, boarding.
A list of tracks to switch between and info about them.
Buttons to trigger actions. (Scan track)

The way to highlight violations could be similar to papers please.
Start the "highlight mode?" select a rule on the left, select the violation on the right.

Comms screen could also have a dedicated part of the screen, perhaps within the right panel to understand the progress of the conversation with the track.

----

Optionally, we could go for a terminal-mostly vibe to make it feel cooler.
eg. Retro-future themed terminal-punk space styling. AI and compute is limited. Some of the gameplay is almost typer-challenge-like.


## Progression

There could be upgrades that the player acquires to speed up their work:
- Better scanners to scan more quickly/accurately

- Different types of scanners to check for cargo, crew, component status, ranges, accuracy.

- Maybe the player could leverage acquired/salvaged components from other ships?

- The player could be charged with the control of vessels, and they must outfit and maintain them.
